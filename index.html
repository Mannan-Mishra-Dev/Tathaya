Process

How it works

Every claim goes through the same five-step check, so the verdict is repeatable, not a guess.

01

Submit

Paste a link, a claim, or upload a screenshot.

02

Extract claims

Tathya isolates the specific factual assertions.

03

Analyze sources

Cross-checks against credible, ranked sources.

04

Compare evidence

Weighs evidence for and against side by side.

05

Generate verdict

Returns a rated verdict with full evidence trail.

Truth spectrum

Every verdict, clearly labeled

No single "true or false" button. Claims land somewhere on a spectrum, with evidence for both sides.

True

Backed by credible, corroborating sources.

False

Contradicted by verifiable evidence.

Misleading

Technically accurate, but framed to deceive.

Unverified

Not enough credible evidence either way.
