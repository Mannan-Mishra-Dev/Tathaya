How Tathya works

Five steps from a submitted claim to a fully-sourced verdict.

01

Submit

Paste a link, upload media, or describe the claim in the chat.

02

Extract claims

The system identifies the specific factual claims being made.

03

Analyze sources

Each claim is checked against credible, cross-referenced sources.

04

Compare evidence

Evidence supporting and refuting the claim is gathered side by side.

05

Generate verdict

A rating and full evidence trail is returned — not just a single label.

The Truth Spectrum

Every verdict lands on one of four ratings.

True

Well-supported by credible sources.

False

Contradicted by credible evidence.

Misleading

Partly accurate but missing key context.

Unverified

Not enough evidence to confirm either way.
